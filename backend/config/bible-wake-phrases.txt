# Smart Bible wake phrases — natural scripture-introduction cues the preacher already says.
# Tokenize into keywords.txt with:
#   python3 backend/scripts/kws_text2token.py <bpe.model> backend/config/bible-wake-phrases.txt > backend/config/bible-keywords.txt
#
# Format per line:  PHRASE  <tab> boost(optional)  <tab> threshold(optional)
#   - boost     : per-phrase score bump (higher = easier to fire). Blank = model default.
#   - threshold : per-phrase min score (higher = fewer false wakes). Blank = global default.
# False wakes are cheap by design (they open an invisible window; parseForProject still gates
# the screen), so bias toward recall. TUNE thresholds against a real recorded service (Phase #7).
#
# Lines starting with '#' and blank lines are ignored.

# --- Distinctive multi-word cues (specific → low threshold / default) ---

OPEN YOUR BIBLES
TURN WITH ME TO
TURN IN YOUR BIBLE TO
TURN IN YOUR BIBLES TO
LET US TURN TO
LET'S TURN TO
THE BOOK OF
IT IS WRITTEN
OUR SCRIPTURE READING
THE SCRIPTURE READS
HEAR THE WORD OF THE LORD
